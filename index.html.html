Construction Corner (“we”, “us”, “our”, or the “App”) connects people in the construction industry — clients, skilled workers, contractors and professionals — so they can discover one another, communicate, and record verified work. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Construction Corner, you agree to this policy.

The App is operated by Naitik Rathod, based in India.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account details: your name, phone number and/or email address used to sign in.
  • Profile information: depending on your role, this may include your trade or profession, skills, years of experience, availability, bio, team size, business details, and identifiers you choose to add such as GST number or Aadhaar / registration details for verification.
  • Photos and files: profile photos, work/gallery/project photos, and documents you upload or share in chat.
  • Work records: details of jobs you create or confirm, including project descriptions, amounts, dates, ratings and reviews.
  • Messages and enquiries: the content of chats and enquiries you send through the App.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: with your permission, your device’s location to set your current working location and to power “near me” search and distance filtering. You can decline and enter your location manually.
  • Usage and device data: basic technical information such as device type and app activity, used to keep the App working and to fix problems.
We only collect location and camera/photo access when you grant permission. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and profile.
  • To let clients discover and contact workers, contractors and professionals, and vice versa.
  • To power search, category browsing, and location-based results.
  • To enable chat, enquiries, and the creation and confirmation of verified work records.
  • To display ratings, reviews and verified work on profiles.
  • To send you notifications relevant to your activity (e.g. new messages, work confirmations).
  • To maintain the safety, security and proper functioning of the App.

3. How Information Is Shared

Construction Corner is a networking platform, so some information is shared by design:

  • Public profile information (such as your name, role, skills, location area, ratings, reviews and verified work) is visible to other users so they can decide whether to contact or hire you.
  • Messages and enquiries are shared with the person you send them to.
  • Verified work records and reviews are visible to the parties involved and reflected on profiles.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the App (for example, secure cloud hosting and database services), and where required by law.

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Storage) to run the App’s accounts, database and file storage. Your information is processed on these services in line with Google’s security practices.

4. Payments

Where the App facilitates payments (for example, platform charges on verified work), those transactions are handled by third-party payment providers. We do not store your full card or bank details; they are processed securely by the payment provider. [Update or remove this section once your payment flow is finalised.]

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and update: you can view and edit your profile information in the App at any time.
  • Permissions: you can grant or withdraw location, camera and photo permissions in your device settings.
  • Delete your account: you can delete your account from within the App, which removes your profile and associated personal data, subject to any records we must retain by law.
  • Notifications: you can manage notification preferences in the App or your device settings.

6. How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your Construction Corner account and associated personal data at any time, directly within the app:

  • Open the Construction Corner app and sign in.
  • Go to Settings.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  • Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Deleting your account removes your profile, photos, and associated personal data from our systems. Certain records may be retained where required to comply with legal obligations or to resolve disputes, as described below.

If you are unable to access the app or need help deleting your account, email us at naitik.build@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account", and we will process your request.

7. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. When you delete your account, we remove your personal data, except where we are required to keep certain information to comply with legal obligations or to resolve disputes.

8. Children

Construction Corner is intended for adults working in or hiring for the construction industry. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy
